# Recommended resources

These readings and references support the *Surveying the Ring Chemistry of
ansa-Sandwich Complexes* project. They cover the organometallic background
you will need to recognize the complexes and classify their ring systems,
plus practical tools for building the dataset and the bar chart.

## Background on sandwich and ansa-sandwich complexes

**An organometallic chemistry textbook**

Any standard organometallic chemistry text will introduce sandwich
complexes and metallocenes. Good options include:

- Robert H. Crabtree, *The Organometallic Chemistry of the Transition
  Metals* — clear introduction to metallocenes and ligand types.
- Christoph Elschenbroich, *Organometallics* — broad coverage of sandwich
  compounds, including less common ring systems.

Focus on the chapters introducing:

- metallocenes and the cyclopentadienyl (Cp) ligand
- arene and other ring ligands as sandwich decks
- bent and bridged (*ansa*) metallocenes

**ansa-Metallocene review literature**

Look for review articles on *ansa*-metallocenes and bridged sandwich
complexes (for example, reviews in *Chemical Reviews* or
*Coordination Chemistry Reviews*). These are the richest source of named
complexes for your survey and often tabulate the ring systems used.

## Finding and recording complexes

- **Wikipedia: [Sandwich compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandwich_compound)
  and [Metallocene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallocene)** — a fast
  orientation to the vocabulary and common examples before you go to the
  primary literature.
- When you find a complex, record where you found it. Keeping a source
  column in your dataset makes the survey reproducible.

## Tools for the dataset and chart

- **Python**, with:
  - **pandas** for building and manipulating your tidy table
    ([pandas docs](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/))
  - **matplotlib** for the bar chart
    ([matplotlib bar chart docs](https://matplotlib.org/stable/api/_as_gen/matplotlib.pyplot.bar.html))
- A plain **CSV file** is a perfectly good format for the survey dataset —
  it is easy to edit by hand and easy to read back into pandas.

## A note on scope

You will not find a single authoritative list of every ansa-sandwich
complex ever made. That is expected. The goal is a well-defined,
clearly-documented *sample* — not a complete census. Be transparent in your
write-up about how you chose what to include.
